## English (en-us)

### Short description

One widget by the clock for your PC, your home lab and your electricity price. Watch CPU, network and audio, see Docker, Proxmox, Home Assistant and Cloudflare, and plan around power prices in 26 countries. Free, open source, no ads or telemetry.

### Description

```
Your PC, your home lab and the electricity price – at a glance, right by the clock.

LabWidge is a small dashboard for Windows that answers the questions you would otherwise open five apps for: How is my PC doing? Are my servers and containers running? Which speakers are on? Is power cheap right now? Turn on the plugins you use – the rest stays out of your way.

KEEP AN EYE ON YOUR PC
CPU with a live chart, RAM, every disk and the graphics card – NVIDIA cards also show temperature, power draw and fan speed. External and local IP, gateway, DNS, ping and network traffic; click an address to copy it.

SWITCH AUDIO IN ONE CLICK
One button per output – speakers, headset, monitor – the volume on the scroll wheel, a microphone mute button and the battery level of supported wireless headsets.

YOUR HOME LAB ON THE DESKTOP
• Docker through Portainer: the containers on all your hosts – which run, which stopped and which are unhealthy.
• Proxmox VE: CPU, RAM and storage of your server, and start, shut down or reboot VMs and containers.
• Home Assistant: the lights, switches and sensors you choose – or a full Home Assistant dashboard in a window.
• Cloudflare: your DNS records, and whether your tunnels and the services behind them are up.
The integrations are optional and use your own servers and access tokens.

SAVE ON ELECTRICITY
The price right now with a chart for today and tomorrow and the cheapest periods – in quarter-hours where the market has them – in 26 European countries. Get a heads-up before power gets cheap or expensive, find the cheapest time to start the dishwasher, the washing machine or the car, and let Home Assistant switch things on and off by the price. In Denmark it is your real price including grid tariff, taxes, your supplier's add-on and VAT. Price history stays on your PC and can be exported as CSV.

YOUR DESKTOP, YOUR WAY
Drag sections out as windows of their own and pin them, move and collapse sections, and open your favourite programs and websites from the shortcut rail. Light or dark theme, seven colour presets, your choice of font and text size, opacity, and English or Danish.

LabWidge is free and open source under the MIT license. No ads, no account and no telemetry. The app only contacts the services your active plugins use, and access tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager. See the privacy policy for details.

Requires Windows 10/11 on an x64 PC. Electricity prices and online services need an internet connection; electricity prices are estimates based on your settings and the data source.
```

### Product features

1. PC monitoring: CPU, RAM, disks and graphics card, with temperature on NVIDIA cards.
2. Network: external and local IP, ping and traffic – click an address to copy it.
3. Switch audio outputs in one click, adjust the volume and mute your microphone.
4. Docker through Portainer: the containers on all your hosts, including stopped and unhealthy ones.
5. Proxmox VE: server status, and start, shut down or reboot VMs and containers.
6. Home Assistant: control lights and switches, or show a full dashboard.
7. Cloudflare: DNS records and the status of your tunnels and the services behind them.
8. Electricity prices in 26 countries with a chart, the cheapest periods and notifications.
9. Plan appliances around the price and let Home Assistant switch them on automatically.
10. Detachable windows, shortcuts, themes, colour presets and your choice of font.
11. Free and open source, with no ads, account or telemetry.

## Dansk (da-dk)

### Kort beskrivelse

En samlet widget ved uret til din pc, dit homelab og din elpris. Følg CPU, netværk og lyd, se Docker, Proxmox, Home Assistant og Cloudflare, og planlæg efter elprisen i 26 lande. Gratis, open source og uden reklamer eller telemetri.

```
Din pc, dit homelab og elprisen – samlet ved uret.

LabWidge er et lille dashboard til Windows, der svarer på de spørgsmål, du ellers åbner fem programmer for: Hvordan har pc'en det? Kører mine servere og containere? Hvilke højttalere er valgt? Er strømmen billig lige nu? Slå de plugins til, du bruger – resten holder sig ude af vejen.

HOLD ØJE MED DIN PC
```
